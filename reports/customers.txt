  CLV_3y = realised net profit + mean net profit per order x ORDERS_PER_YEAR x sum(r^t, t=1..3), r=0.8
  total_orders_by_customer was NOT used: it averages 25.5 orders per customer, contradicting the 1-order reality, and its derived order rate has correlation 0.0019 with actual profit (noise).
